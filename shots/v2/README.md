# The store, V2

Each room in turn, from the barest (fewest triangles) up: what was too primitive, made again. Before on the left, after
on the right. Each one is a section of `tools/remodel.gd`, run on `world/world.scn`.

## The back hall (`hall`)

A rubber base along the walls; the break room's and the restroom's doors panelled, with kick plates and hinges; the
ceiling lights framed; an extinguisher and its sign; a lit EXIT over the way back to the store; the staff's corkboard.

## The roof (`roof`)

The tall block's blank wall over the store's roof given what a wall like that has: the projection booth's exhaust
louvre, two scuppers off its roof with their downspouts and splash blocks, a wall pack, a disconnect and its conduit.
A satellite dish on a ballasted mount by the west parapet. Next door's rooftop unit (a plain box) given louvres, a fan
in its guard, rails and a disconnect.

## The break room (`breakroom`)

Already well furnished; what was wrong with it: the tall block's base course ran on along its side through the wall, a
grey kerb along the room's west wall (now it stops where the back rooms do). The table's top banded in chrome. A
microwave on its cart in the corner (door, window, keypad, handle; plates and a roll of towels under it).

## The sales floor: its TVs and ceiling (`aisles`)

The thirteen hanging TVs (a box, a smaller box behind it, a screen) made CRTs: the tube's housing tapering back to its
neck, a bezel round the picture with a badge and a power light, vents along the top, a mounting plate behind. The
ceiling given its sprinkler heads, a grid of them kept clear of the light panels. (The shelving itself is laid out by
the game as it opens, not the level: `store.gd`.)
